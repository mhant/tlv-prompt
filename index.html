Episode Briefing

In this premiere episode of The TLV Prompt, George K. Stein sits down with veteran ecosystem organizer Ofir Leitner, founder of the Israel Mobile Summit and Game Changer Tel Aviv (focusing on GenAI and gaming, heading into its 3rd edition in 2027).

Leitner shares the unvarnished operational realities of staging premier technology summits amidst regional conflict: weathering the 2024 summit where international attendance plummeted from 4,500 down to 20, executing a 3-week postponement in October 2025 following direct Iranian ballistic missile barrages while miraculously retaining 90–95% of corporate sponsors, and steadfastly rejecting relocations to Cyprus or hybrid Zoom compromises.

Leitner and Stein analyze the underlying economics of modern tech gatherings—explaining why commercial flight grounding and personal safety concerns, rather than corporate boycotts (<5%), drive attendance drops, how generative AI is transforming mobile monetization pipelines while leaving human B2B relationships untouched, and how Israeli tech founders navigate shekel-dollar currency squeezes and tech layoffs ahead of the next Israel Mobile Summit on October 29, 2026 at Expo Tel Aviv.

Featured Guest: Ofir Leitner
Founder and Managing Director of the Israel Mobile Summit (one of EMEA's longest-running mobile and app ecosystems) and Game Changer Tel Aviv. A 15-year veteran of tech summit production, mobile growth, and gaming infrastructure.
